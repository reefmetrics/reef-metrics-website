A Spectrophotometer, Not a Guess.

At Wavie's core is a multi-wavelength optical bench. Three narrow-band LEDs — 540 nm green, 615 nm orange, and 880 nm near-infrared — shine through the sample cell one at a time while a high-dynamic-range light sensor reads exactly how much makes it through. It's the same colorimetric method a lab uses, automated down to a footprint that fits your cabinet. No color cards, no eyeballing, no drift between one test and the next.

Precision Engineering. Every Detail.

Built by reef hobbyists who know what matters. A single bidirectional pump meters every fluid to hundredths of a milliliter, and a purpose-built rotary valve routes eight fluid paths from one compact head — not an off-the-shelf lab instrument dressed up for aquariums. Every component was chosen with your sump in mind.

Smaller Than Anything Else.

Competing systems are bulky by design. Wavie was built compact-first — it tucks into a sump cabinet, sits on a shelf, or mounts to a wall. Everything above lives inside a single sealed tower barely taller than a banana. No compromises on capability. Just less space and a cleaner setup.

Everything Plugs Into the Back.

One rear panel carries it all — 24V power, the CAN expansion bus, and isolated inputs for every probe: temperature, pH, ORP, salinity, and dissolved oxygen. Each connector is labeled and keyed, so wiring Wavie in is plug-and-go. That CAN bus is exactly how Wavie Power and Dose join the system later — no new run back to your controller.

Make It Yours.

A ring of addressable RGB LEDs wraps the base and backlights the ReefMetrics logo. Pick any color, set the brightness, and match your tank's mood — and let it react to what Wavie is doing. It's the finishing touch on a system built to showcase.

Under the Hood

Engineered in Three Tiers

Optics and pump up top, fluidic selection in the middle, electronics at the base — every subsystem purpose-built and packed tight.

Custom Mainboard

An ESP32-S3 controller — Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, 16 MB storage and 8 MB RAM — with three galvanically isolated probe front-ends, dual stepper drivers, and an onboard CAN transceiver, all designed and laid out in-house.

Rotary Fluidics

A single peristaltic pump and a custom rotary valve move every fluid — six reagents, sample, and waste — through one shared path.

Status at a Glance

An addressable LED ring shows exactly what Wavie is doing — priming, testing, or reporting — from across the room.
