Three tiers.

Every part comes out.

Pump, optics, valve and boards are separate modules, so one can be repaired or replaced without the rest.

The optical bench

Three LEDs at 540, 615 and 880 nm shine through a clear cuvette onto a light sensor. How much light the sample absorbs becomes the reading.

The pump

One peristaltic pump meters the tank sample and every reagent through a single path, in both directions.

The rotary valve

A stepper-driven valve selects one of eight ports: six reagents, the sample and waste. It homes itself before every run.

The mainboard

One custom board runs the instrument on an ESP32-S3 with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. It carries isolated probe inputs, two stepper drivers, a CAN bus for expansion and USB-C for recovery.

  • 24 V power in
  • CAN bus for Wavie Dose and Wavie Power
  • USB-C for firmware recovery
  • pH, ORP and dissolved oxygen probe inputs, each isolated
  • Salinity probe input
  • Temperature probe input

The RGB ring

Thirty-three LEDs under a diffuser, and a separately lit logo. Set them to any colour, or let them show what Wavie is doing.

And it all goes back.

Open hardware

Wavie is built to be opened, understood and repaired by the person who owns it.

The design files and documentation are being published on GitHub under a non-commercial licence: build, repair and modify your own, but not for resale. The wiki explains how each part works in far more detail than this page.

We will sell replacement parts at cost. Reagents are formulated and sold only by ReefMetrics, which is what keeps the project running.

Questions

What is the Beta Kit?

It is the first production run of Wavie, sold before the project is finished to the people who want to help finish it. You get the tester, four probes and reagents. You also get early hardware, unfinished software and no warranty.

When will it ship?

Our estimate is the first half of 2027, between Q1 and Q2. That depends on the project being properly funded, and the date could move. We will post progress on Discord.

Can I get a refund?

No. Pre-orders pay for the work of completing Wavie, so the money is spent on development rather than held. Please only order if you are comfortable with that. The Refund and Warranty Policy has the detail.

Is there a warranty?

The Beta Kit has no warranty. Wavie is designed to be repaired by its owner: the design files and wiki are there to help, and we will sell replacement parts at cost.

Are the reagents ready?

Not yet. The reagent chemistry has not been formulated, and it is the largest piece of work still ahead. The Beta Kit ships with reagents in the box. Reagents are made and sold only by ReefMetrics.

Which probes are included?

pH, ORP, salinity and temperature. Wavie also has an input for dissolved oxygen; that probe will be sold separately in our store at launch.

What does open hardware mean here?

The design files and documentation for Wavie are published so owners can understand, repair and modify their own unit. The licence is non-commercial, so the designs cannot be used to make units for sale. The reagent formulations are not published.

What about Wavie Dose and Wavie Power?

Both are still planned and Wavie already has the CAN connection they will use. Neither has a confirmed release date or price.

Where can I follow progress?

On our Discord. Build updates are posted there, and it is the fastest way to ask us something.
