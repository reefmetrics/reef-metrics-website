A Spectrophotometer, Not a Guess.

At Wavie's core is a multi-wavelength optical bench. Calibrated LEDs illuminate the sample cell and a precision light sensor reads absorbance directly — the same colorimetric method a lab uses, automated down to a footprint that fits your cabinet. No color cards, no eyeballing, no drift between one test and the next.

Precision Engineering. Every Detail.

Built by reef hobbyists who know what matters. A single bidirectional pump meters every fluid to hundredths of a milliliter, and a purpose-built rotary valve routes eight fluid paths from one compact head — not an off-the-shelf lab instrument dressed up for aquariums. Every component was chosen with your sump in mind.

Smaller Than Anything Else.

Competing systems are bulky by design. Wavie was built compact-first — it tucks into a sump cabinet, sits on a shelf, or mounts to a wall. Everything above lives inside a single sealed tower barely taller than a banana. No compromises on capability. Just less space and a cleaner setup.

Under the Hood

Engineered in Three Tiers

Optics up top, fluidics in the middle, electronics at the base — every subsystem purpose-built and packed tight.

Custom Mainboard

An ESP32-S3 controller with isolated probe front-ends, dual motor drivers, and Wi-Fi — designed and laid out in-house.

Rotary Fluidics

A single peristaltic pump and a custom rotary valve move every fluid — six reagents, sample, and waste — through one shared path.

Status at a Glance

An addressable LED ring shows exactly what Wavie is doing — priming, testing, or reporting — from across the room.
