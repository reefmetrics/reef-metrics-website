Refund & Warranty Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Beta waitlist deposit — non-refundable

The $500 beta waitlist deposit is non-refundable. It directly funds the final development of Wavie and reserves your place in the launch order. What you get in return is real: the full $500 is credited back to you as a one-time $500 code valid toward any ReefMetrics product at release (Wavie, Wavie Power, or Wavie Dose). See the Beta Waitlist Terms for full details on the deposit and credit code.

Your deposit isn't lost money — it becomes a $500 credit toward your purchase. It simply can't be withdrawn as a cash refund.

2. Hardware warranty

Every Wavie, Wavie Power, and Wavie Dose unit is covered by a 30-day limited warranty from the date you receive it. If your hardware arrives defective or fails due to a manufacturing fault within 30 days, we'll repair or replace it at no cost to you.

3. Free return shipping

For any valid warranty claim within the 30-day window, we cover return shipping both ways. You won't pay to send a defective unit back or to receive its repair or replacement.

4. What's covered

  • Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship.
  • Hardware that fails to function as described under normal aquarium use.
  • Units that arrive damaged in transit (report within 30 days of delivery).

5. What's not covered

  • Damage from misuse, accident, modification, or use outside the product's guidelines.
  • Normal wear of consumables such as reagents and tubing.
  • Damage caused by using non-ReefMetrics reagents or parts where ReefMetrics parts are specified.

6. How to make a claim

Email support@reef-metrics.com with your order details and a short description of the issue (photos or a short video help). We'll respond quickly with next steps and a prepaid return label if a return is needed.

7. Contact

Questions about refunds or warranty coverage? Reach us anytime at support@reef-metrics.com.
