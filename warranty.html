Refund and Warranty Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Beta Kit pre-orders are non-refundable, and the Beta Kit has no warranty. We sell replacement parts at cost so you can keep yours running.

1. Pre-orders are non-refundable

A Beta Kit pre-order pays for the work of completing Wavie. The money is spent on development, parts and reagent chemistry rather than held, so it cannot be refunded once paid.

Please order only if you are comfortable funding a project that is still in development, with an estimated ship date of Q1–Q2 2027 that depends on funding. The Pre-order Terms set this out in full.

2. The Beta Kit has no warranty

The Beta Kit is early hardware. It is supplied without a warranty, "as is", to the fullest extent the law allows. We do not offer free repair, replacement or return shipping for Beta Kits.

3. Repairs and parts

Wavie is designed to be opened and repaired by its owner. To make that practical:

  • the hardware design files and documentation are published on GitHub;
  • the Wavie wiki explains how each part works;
  • we will sell replacement parts at cost.

4. Damage in shipping

If your kit arrives damaged, email support@reef-metrics.com with photos of the packaging and the unit as soon as you can, so we can help you with a claim against the carrier.

5. Reagents, the dissolved oxygen probe and later products

Reagent refills and the dissolved oxygen probe will be sold in our store at launch. Wavie Dose and Wavie Power are planned but have no confirmed release. The refund and warranty terms for anything sold in the store will be published when the store opens.

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not remove any right you have under the law of the place you live if that right cannot be waived by agreement.

7. Contact

Email support@reef-metrics.com with any question about this policy.

Wavie is open hardware. The documentation and design files live on GitHub.
