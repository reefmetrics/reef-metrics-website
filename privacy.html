Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains what information ReefMetrics collects when you join the Wavie beta waitlist or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this simple because our data practices are simple.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact & shipping details you provide at checkout: name, email address, shipping address, and phone number.
  • Order information: the fact that you placed a $500 deposit, and the credit code issued to you.
  • Messages you send us by email.

We do not collect or store your card number or payment details — those go directly to our payment processor (see below).

2. How we use it

  • To process your deposit and issue your $500 credit code.
  • To ship your product and provide support and warranty service.
  • To email you about the beta program, ordering, and product updates.
  • To keep the waitlist in order so we can fulfill fairly.

3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the waitlist. We share only what each needs:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe collects and handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend / Amazon SES — to deliver your confirmation and update emails.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages — website hosting and DNS.
  • Google Workspace — our support and contact email.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

4. Cookies & tracking

Our site is static and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting providers may process basic, aggregated request data (such as IP address and page requests) to serve the site securely.

5. Data retention

We keep your waitlist and order information for as long as needed to fulfill the program, provide warranty service, and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete information we're not required to keep.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or unsubscribe from non-essential emails, at any time. Email support@reef-metrics.com and we'll take care of it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product and program grow. Changes are posted here with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support@reef-metrics.com.
