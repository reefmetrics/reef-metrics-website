Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

We collect what we need to take your pre-order, ship your kit and answer your email. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

  • Contact and shipping details you give at checkout: name, email address, shipping address and phone number.
  • Order information: what you ordered, when, and what you paid.
  • Messages you send us by email.

We do not collect or store your card number. Payment details go directly to our payment processor.

2. How we use it

  • To process your pre-order and send you its confirmation.
  • To ship your Beta Kit and to help you with it afterwards.
  • To email you about the progress of the project and your order.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what each of these service providers needs to do its job:

  • Stripe processes payments and handles your payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages host this website and its DNS.
  • Google provides our support email and serves the typeface used on this site.
  • Shipping carriers receive your name and address to deliver your kit.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting providers and Google Fonts process basic request data, such as your IP address, in order to serve the pages. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page.

5. Discord and GitHub

Our community runs on Discord and our documentation is on GitHub. If you use them, those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as we need it to deliver your kit, support it, and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop receiving progress emails, at any time. Email support@reef-metrics.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project grows. Changes are posted here with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support@reef-metrics.com.
