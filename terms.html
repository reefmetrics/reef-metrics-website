Pre-order Terms

Last updated October 13, 2026

Pre-ordering a Wavie Beta Kit pays for the work of finishing Wavie. Your payment is non-refundable, the ship date is an estimate, and the Beta Kit has no warranty.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms apply when you pre-order a Wavie Beta Kit from ReefMetrics ("we", "us"). By placing a pre-order you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not order.

2. What you are ordering

One Wavie Beta Kit, which contains:

  • the Wavie automated reef water tester;
  • four probes: pH, ORP, salinity and temperature;
  • reagents, shipped in the box with the kit.

The dissolved oxygen probe is not included. It will be sold separately in our store at launch. Wavie Dose and Wavie Power are separate planned products and are not part of this pre-order.

3. Price and payment

The Beta Kit costs $1,999.99 USD. Payment is taken in full when you order, through Stripe. Any sales tax that applies is shown at checkout before you pay.

We ship to the United States and Canada, and shipping is included in the price. For orders to Canada, any import duties and taxes are paid by the recipient.

4. Your payment funds the project and is non-refundable

Wavie is not finished. Pre-order payments are spent on completing it: engineering, parts, tooling and reagent development. Because the money is used rather than held, pre-orders are non-refundable once paid, and cannot be cancelled or transferred for cash.

Nothing in these terms removes a right you have under the law of the place you live if that right cannot be waived by agreement.

5. The state of the project

You are ordering a product that is still in development. In particular:

  • The reagents have not been formulated yet. The chemistry that produces the alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate and phosphate readings is still being developed.
  • The hardware, software, features and specifications described on this site may change before the kit ships.
  • The list of "planned functionality" describes what we intend to build. Some of it may arrive after the kit ships, may change, or may not be delivered.
  • Delivery depends on the project being properly funded. There is a risk of delay, and a risk that the project cannot be completed as planned.

6. Estimated delivery

We estimate that Beta Kits will ship between the first and second quarter of 2027 (Q1–Q2 2027). This is an estimate and not a promise. It depends on funding and on development going to plan, and it may move. We will post progress on Discord and email you before your kit ships.

7. No warranty

The Beta Kit is early hardware and is supplied without a warranty, "as is", to the fullest extent the law allows. Wavie is designed to be repaired by its owner: we publish the design files and documentation, and we will sell replacement parts at cost. See the Refund and Warranty Policy.

8. Using a Beta Kit

Beta hardware and software can fail or give wrong readings. You remain responsible for your aquarium and its livestock. Do not rely on Wavie alone for decisions that could harm them, and confirm any reading that matters with another method.

Reagents are chemicals. Follow the handling and storage instructions supplied with them.

Reagent formulations are not published. Reagents for Wavie are made and sold only by ReefMetrics.

10. Limit of our liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, our total liability to you in connection with a pre-order is limited to the amount you paid for it, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of livestock.
