Questions

What is the Beta Kit?

It is the first production run of Wavie, sold before the project is finished to the people who want to help finish it. You get the tester, four probes and reagents. You also get early hardware, unfinished software and no warranty.

When will it ship?

Our estimate is the first half of 2027, between Q1 and Q2. That depends on the project being properly funded, and the date could move. We will post progress on Discord.

Where do you ship, and what does it cost?

We ship Beta Kits to the US and Canada, and shipping is included in the price. Payment is taken in full at checkout by Stripe, with sales tax added where it applies. For orders to Canada, import duties and taxes are charged on delivery and are paid by the recipient.

Can I get a refund?

No. Pre-orders pay for the work of completing Wavie, so the money is spent on development rather than held. Please only order if you are comfortable with that. If we cancel the project before kits ship, we refund the pre-order money that remains, shared in proportion to what each person paid. The Refund and Warranty Policy has the detail.

Is there a warranty?

The Beta Kit has no warranty. Wavie is designed to be repaired by its owner: the design files and wiki are there to help, and we will sell replacement parts at cost.

Are the reagents ready?

Not yet. Formulation has started and is in development; it is the largest piece of work still ahead. The Beta Kit ships with six reagent bottles, which sit beside Wavie in a tray (the tray design is not final). Reagents are made by ReefMetrics and will be offered as a subscription.

Is there a launch offer?

Yes. The first 50 Beta Kit pre-orders get 12 months of the reagent subscription free, if Beta Kits ship and the subscription goes on sale. It starts when your kit ships or the subscription opens, whichever is later. If the project is cancelled, the offer does not apply. The Pre-order Terms have the full conditions.

Which probes are included?

pH, ORP, salinity and temperature, with calibration fluids. The app walks you through calibrating them. Wavie also has an input for dissolved oxygen; that probe will be sold separately in our store at launch.

What comes in the box?

Wavie and its power supply, the four probes with their calibration fluids, and six reagent bottles that sit beside Wavie in a tray.

What does open hardware mean here?

The design files and documentation for Wavie are published so owners can understand, repair and modify their own unit. The licence is non-commercial, so the designs cannot be used to make units for sale. The reagent formulations are not published today; if the project gains traction, we may publish them in future.

What about Wavie Dose and Wavie Power?

Both are still planned and Wavie already has the CAN connection they will use. Neither has a confirmed release date or price.

Where can I follow progress?

On our Discord. Build updates are posted there, and it is the fastest way to ask us something.

Wavie is open hardware. The documentation and design files live on GitHub.
