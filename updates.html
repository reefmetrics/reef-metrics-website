Updates

What is planned for Wavie, what else is coming, and where to follow the build.

Planned functionality

What we are building toward. Some of it will arrive after the Beta Kit ships, through software updates.

  • Automated chemistryAlkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate and phosphate from six reagents.
  • Continuous probe readingspH, ORP, salinity and temperature around the clock.
  • Dissolved oxygenWith the add-on probe; the input for it is built in.
  • Scheduled testingChoose which tests run and how often.
  • Guided calibrationCalibrate the probes step by step in the app, using the fluids in the kit.
  • The ReefMetrics appOne app for the whole ecosystem: readings, trends and alerts. It is still being built.
  • Over-the-air updatesNew features and fixes delivered over Wi-Fi.
  • Custom lightingAny colour for the ring and logo, or status at a glance.
  • USB-C recoveryRe-flash the firmware from a computer if anything goes wrong.
  • Expansion over CANThe connection that Wavie Dose and Wavie Power will use.

Coming soon

Two more devices are planned for the ReefMetrics ecosystem. They connect to Wavie over its CAN bus. Neither is available to order yet, and neither has a release date.

Coming soon

Wavie Dose

Dosing that responds to Wavie's own test results, so the tank is corrected as well as measured.

Coming soon

Wavie Power

Switched power and control for the pumps, lights and heaters around the tank.
