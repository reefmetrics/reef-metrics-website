Features

What Wavie measures, the parts that do it, and everything you can see and plug in.

What it measures

Five parameters by reagent chemistry and five by probe, without test kits or matching colours by eye.

By reagent

  • Alkalinity
  • Calcium
  • Magnesium
  • Nitrate
  • Phosphate

By probe

  • pH
  • ORP
  • Salinity
  • Temperature
  • Dissolved oxygen probe sold separately

The reagents are still in development and have not been formulated yet. Read what is and isn't finished before you order.

The parts

Four modules do the work. Each one comes out on its own.

The optical bench

Three LEDs at 540, 615 and 880 nm shine through a clear cuvette onto a light sensor. How much light the sample absorbs becomes the reading.

The pump

A peristaltic pump meters the tank sample and every reagent through one shared path, in both directions. Liquid only ever touches the tubing.

The rotary valve

A stepper-driven rotor selects one of eight ports: six reagents, the sample and waste. It homes itself before every run.

The mainboard

One custom board runs everything on an ESP32-S3 with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth: isolated probe inputs, two stepper drivers, a CAN bus for expansion and USB-C for recovery.

Lighting

Status you can read from across the room.

The RGB ring

Thirty-three LEDs under a diffuser at the base, and a separately lit crescent logo. Pick any colour for each, or let them show what Wavie is doing: testing, finished, or needs attention.

Small enough to forget about

Wavie stands in a sump cabinet or on a shelf beside the tank.

Open hardware

Wavie is built to be opened, understood and repaired by the person who owns it.

The design files and documentation are being published on GitHub under a non-commercial licence: build, repair and modify your own, but not for resale. The wiki explains how each part works in far more detail than this page.

We will sell replacement parts at cost. Reagents are formulated and sold only by ReefMetrics, which is what keeps the project running.
